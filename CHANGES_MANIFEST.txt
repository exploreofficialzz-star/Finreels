FinReels — files touched this round (the "category content not showing" fix)
================================================================================
Extract into your project root, overwriting existing files at the same paths.

lib/main.dart
    THE actual fix. Split the old single 8-way startup race (Connectivity,
    Background, Notifications, Ads, IAP, ResourceCategoryData, UserProfile,
    Engagement — all in one Future.wait with one 6s ceiling) into two groups:

    - Group A: ResourceCategoryData.load() + UserProfileService.init() +
      EngagementService.init() — the three things FeedProvider's
      constructor reads synchronously. All three are local-only (bundled
      JSON, on-device prefs) so they should always be fast, and
      FeedProvider genuinely cannot be correct without them — so
      FeedProvider() is now ALWAYS constructed after this group resolves
      (own 8s ceiling), full stop.

    - Group B: Connectivity/Background/Notifications/Ads/IAP — can be
      genuinely slow (external SDKs) without that ever being a reason
      FeedProvider should wait. Fully unawaited, own 6s ceiling, runs
      concurrently with Group A, never gates FeedProvider or the
      splash-to-shell transition.

    Previously: if Ads/IAP (or any one of the 8) ran long, the WHOLE group
    could blow past 6s, and FeedProvider() got built immediately after
    regardless — sometimes with ResourceCategoryData/UserProfileService
    still empty. Nothing ever corrected that afterward (ResourceCategoryData
    isn't a ChangeNotifier), so the selected category's content just never
    showed up for that entire session, while general content (hardcoded,
    no async load needed) always worked fine. That mismatch is exactly what
    the screenshots showed.

lib/providers/feed_provider.dart
    Second, smaller fix. _sessionChannelOrder (which decides which channel
    gets boosted to the top of Videos/Shorts) was a `final` field, frozen
    once at FeedProvider construction — which happens BEFORE onboarding is
    even shown for a first-time person, so their just-picked category could
    never get boosted until the NEXT app launch. Now mutable, and
    recomputed inside _onProfileChanged (which already re-fetches on every
    selection change) — so a freshly-selected category is boosted
    immediately, not "starting next time."
